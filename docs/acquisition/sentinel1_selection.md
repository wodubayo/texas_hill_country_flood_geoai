## Key timing finding

The granule dated **2025-07-04** (and listed by the OPERA event service as a July 4 date) was sensed at **00:43 UTC on 4 July = 19:43 CDT on 3 July**, several hours **before** flooding began in the early hours of 4 July. It is the latest pre-event image, not an event image.

No Sentinel-1 acquisition observed the flood peak. The first post-event passes are RO107 on 10 July 19:35 CDT (~6.5 days after, partial AOI) and RO005 on 15 July 19:43 CDT (~11.5 days after, full AOI). Over this flashy upper Guadalupe reach, most overbank water had likely receded by then (to be confirmed with USGS gauges). **The study question therefore shifts from peak inundation extent to persistent post-flood surface change** (scour, sediment/debris deposition, stripped riparian vegetation, lingering ponding, channel change), and validation targets must match that.

## Gauge check (2026-11-17): the RO005 post scene is confounded

Source: `src/check_gauges.py`, USGS NWIS instantaneous values (provisional), raw responses registered under `data/raw/reference/usgs_nwis/`; results in `docs/acquisition/usgs_gauge_scene_check.json`.

- 4 July peaks: Guadalupe at Hunt 234,000 cfs (05:10 CDT), Kerrville 235,000 cfs (06:30), Comfort 142,000 cfs (10:45). Pre-event baseflow was 5-65 cfs (drought).
- **A second flood pulse on 13-15 July** reached every gauge in the AOI: Hunt 7,410 cfs (14 Jul 18:05), Kerrville 4,440 cfs (14 Jul 21:00), Comfort 4,550 cfs (15 Jul 04:15). The RO005 post scene (15 Jul 19:43) was taken on its recession, about one day after the peak.
- Discharge at each sensing time:

| Gauge | RO107 control 06-16 | RO107 pre 06-28 | RO107 post 07-10 | RO005 post 07-15 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Guadalupe at Hunt | 6 | 5 | 194 | 909 |
| Johnson Ck nr Ingram | 8 | 5 | 40 | 837 |
| Guadalupe at Kerrville | 26 | 7 | 209 | 1,990 |
| Guadalupe at Comfort | 95 | 52 | 445 | 2,790 |
| Medina R at Bandera | 10 | 2 | 62 | 571 |

- At the RO005 pre (3 Jul) and control (21 Jun) times, flows were at baseflow (Hunt 7, Kerrville 3-7 cfs).
- The Center Point (08166250) and above-Bear-Creek (08166140) records look unreliable after 4 July (implausibly small peaks, flat values); gauges were likely damaged. Do not use them.

Implication: RO005 pre→post change mixes 4 July after-effects with second-pulse high water and fresh soil wetting (consistent with the +0.76 dB scene-wide VH brightening). RO107 post (10 Jul 19:35) falls in the inter-event low (flows lowest 10-11 Jul) and is the cleaner view of 4 July persistent change, at the cost of 61% AOI coverage (all corridor reference points covered).
